# Product Roadmap

## Phase 0
Specification, architecture, repository, CI, environments, auth and data model.

## Phase 1
Login, RBAC, Android Room, sync queue, API, dashboard and online/offline vertical slice.

## Phase 2
Staffing: clients, jobs, candidates, CVs, interviews, shortlists and placements.

## Phase 3
Learning: programs, courses, cohorts, learners, lessons, assessments, attendance and certificates.

## Phase 4
CRM/Marketing: leads, campaigns, content, follow-ups and analytics.

## Phase 5
AI: document extraction, candidate matching, learning personalization, marketing assistance and management assistant.

## Phase 6
Security, performance, backup/recovery, observability, accessibility and UAT.

## Phase 7
Internal testing, closed testing, production launch and continuous improvement.
